# Daggerheart Character Tracker

A digital tracker and character creation/advancement system strictly faithful to the Daggerheart Core Rules and SRD.

## Language

### Core Character Concepts

**Character**:
The player character (PC) representing a hero in the game, defined by class, heritage, traits, stats, and cards.
_Avoid_: Avatar, toon, hero profile

**Heritage**:
The combination of a character's Ancestry (lineage/physicality) and Community (culture/environment of origin).
_Avoid_: Race, origin, background

**Ancestry**:
The biological or ancestral lineage granting specific physical traits and ancestry features.
_Avoid_: Race, species

**Community**:
The cultural or regional origin of a character granting a community feature.
_Avoid_: Culture, faction

**Trait**:
One of the six core physical, mental, or social attributes: Agility, Strength, Finesse, Instinct, Presence, and Knowledge.
_Avoid_: Ability score, stat, attribute

**Experience**:
A specific word or phrase describing a character's lived background, specialty, or knack, which can be invoked by spending Hope.
_Avoid_: Skill, proficiency, perk

### Cards & Magic

**Domain**:
One of the nine fundamental spheres of power (Arcana, Blade, Bone, Codex, Grace, Midnight, Sage, Splendor, Valor).
_Avoid_: School, magic type, discipline

**Domain Card**:
A specific ability, spell, or grimoire belonging to a domain, associated with a level (1–10) and recall cost.
_Avoid_: Skill card, perk card, spell card

**Loadout**:
The active set of up to 5 domain cards whose mechanical effects the character can use during play.
_Avoid_: Deck, active hand, equipped spells

**Vault**:
The reserve collection of acquired domain cards that are currently inactive and stored away.
_Avoid_: Discard pile, spellbook, graveyard, storage

**Recall Cost**:
The amount of Stress a character must mark to swap a card from their vault into their active loadout outside of a rest.
_Avoid_: Swap cost, stress cost

### Progression & Advancement

**Tier**:
One of the four level groupings of play (Tier 1: Level 1; Tier 2: Levels 2–4; Tier 3: Levels 5–7; Tier 4: Levels 8–10).
_Avoid_: Rank, bracket

**Advancement**:
A specific upgrade slot chosen during level-up (such as increasing traits, adding HP/Stress slots, evasion, domain cards, or subclass upgrades).
_Avoid_: Perk, level reward, talent point

**Tier Achievement**:
Automatic progression milestones granted at levels 2, 5, and 8 (such as gaining a new Experience at +2, +1 Proficiency, and clearing trait marks).
_Avoid_: Milestone bonus, tier perk


### Mechanics & Rules

**Duality Dice**:
The core resolution mechanism consisting of one Hope Die (d12) and one Fear Die (d12) rolled together to determine both success and narrative consequence.
_Avoid_: Action dice, advantage dice

**Recall**:
The action of moving a domain card from the vault into the active loadout during play by spending Stress equal to the card's Recall Cost.
_Avoid_: Swap, summon card, draw

**House Rules**:
A permissive campaign setting enabling players or Game Masters to manually modify stats, thresholds, or card assignments outside standard constraints.
_Avoid_: Cheat mode, god mode, free edit

**Homebrew**:
Custom player- or GM-authored content (such as cards, ancestries, or equipment) formatted to fit the system schema alongside official rules.
_Avoid_: Mod, unofficial content


**Downtime**:
The rest period (Short Rest or Long Rest) during which characters heal, clear stress, repair armor, or freely reorganize their loadout and vault.
_Avoid_: Camp, intermission, break

**Roster**:
The collection of player characters created, saved, and managed locally within the application.
_Avoid_: Character list, party, database

**Theme**:
A configurable JSON specification governing colors, aesthetic accents, and domain card styling across the interface.
_Avoid_: Skin, template, style pack
